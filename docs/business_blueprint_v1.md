# Enterprise Digital Twin
## Business Blueprint V1

### 1. Business Type

Omnichannel Retail Enterprise

### 2. Market

Vietnam

### 3. Business Model

The enterprise sells products through:

- Website
- Mobile App
- Marketplace
- Physical Stores

### 4. Core Business Domains

1. Customer
2. Product
3. Sales
4. Payment
5. Inventory
6. Procurement
7. Warehouse
8. Logistics
9. Marketing
10. Customer Service
11. Finance

### 5. Digital Twin Objective

Simulate realistic enterprise data and business behavior
over time.

### 6. AI Analyst Objective

The AI should:

1. Observe the enterprise
2. Detect anomalies
3. Investigate evidence
4. Identify root causes
5. Forecast consequences
6. Recommend actions

### 7. Evaluation

The system contains hidden:

- Ground Truth
- Root Causes
- Causal Graphs
- Incident Scenarios

These must not be directly accessible to the AI Analyst.

### 8. Multi-Agent Architecture

Three independent analyst agents:

- Sales/Finance Analyst
- Supply Chain/Operations Analyst
- Customer/Market Analyst

Two critic agents:

- Evidence Critic
- Causal Critic

One synthesis agent:

- Final Synthesizer
